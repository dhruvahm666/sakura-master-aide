# 🌸 Sakura — Personal AI Agent

> My personal AI assistant built from scratch to manage my life, knowledge, and daily routine

---

## 📌 What is Sakura?

Sakura is a custom-built personal AI agent designed to act as a smart 
life manager and assistant. She handles everything from daily scheduling 
to decision making, current affairs, and personal advice — all in one place.

Think of her as a personal manager, scheduler, and knowledge companion 
— built entirely by me for my own use.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Daily Event Management** — Keeps track of daily events and important tasks
- 🗓️ **Timetable Scheduling** — Plans and manages my daily routine and schedule
- 📰 **Current Affairs** — Provides up to date news and current affairs
- 💬 **Conversational Chat** — Natural conversation and assistance anytime
- 🧠 **Decision Making Support** — Helps think through decisions with AI reasoning
- 💊 **Medical & Supplement Advice** — Provides guidance on health, supplements, and medications
- 🌱 **Life Advice** — Offers thoughtful personal guidance and motivation
- 📚 **Knowledge Companion** — Helps explore and learn new topics on demand

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Language:** TypeScript
- **AI:** Anthropic Claude API
- **Platform:** Custom built from scratch
- **Purpose:** Personal use and continuous learning

---

## 💡 Why I Built Sakura

I wanted a single intelligent system that could manage my life the way 
a real personal manager would — not just answer questions, but actively 
help me plan, learn, and grow every single day.

Sakura is the result of combining my passion for AI with a real personal 
need for a smarter daily workflow.

---

## 🚀 Key Use Cases

1. Wake up → Check today's schedule with Sakura
2. Stay updated → Ask Sakura for current affairs
3. Plan study sessions → Let Sakura build my timetable
4. Make decisions → Discuss options with Sakura
5. Wind down → Get life advice and reflect on the day
