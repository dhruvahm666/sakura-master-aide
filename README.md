## 📌 What is Sakura?

Sakura is a custom-built personal AI agent designed to act as a smart life manager and assistant. She handles everything from daily scheduling to decision making, current affairs, and personal advice — all in one place.

Think of her as a personal manager, scheduler, and knowledge companion — built by me, for my own use.

---

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 📅 Daily Event Management | Keeps track of daily events and important tasks |
| 🗓️ Timetable Scheduling | Plans and manages my daily routine and schedule |
| 📰 Current Affairs | Provides up to date news and current affairs |
| 💬 Conversational Chat | Natural conversation and assistance anytime |
| 🧠 Decision Making Support | Helps think through decisions with AI reasoning |
| ❤️ General Wellness Tips | Offers general health and wellness suggestions |
| 🌱 Life Advice | Offers thoughtful personal guidance and motivation |
| 📚 Knowledge Companion | Helps explore and learn new topics on demand |

## 💡 Why I Built Sakura

I wanted a single intelligent system that could manage my life the way a real personal manager would — not just answer questions, but actively help me plan, learn, and grow every single day.

Sakura is the result of combining my passion for AI with a real personal need for a smarter daily workflow.

---

## 🚀 Key Use Cases

> **Morning** — Wake up and check today's full schedule with Sakura

> **Stay Informed** — Ask Sakura for current affairs and daily news

> **Study Planning** — Let Sakura build and manage my timetable

> **Decision Making** — Discuss options and think through choices with Sakura

> **Wind Down** — Get life advice, wellness tips, and reflect on the day
